Good Day Goods is a small independent brand idea built around everyday clothes and positive messages. We're starting with one shirt, learning as we go, and keeping the whole thing simple.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Can I order a shirt right now?

Not yet. This page is a design prototype, and the bag is only a demo. Payment, shipping, and order handling still need to be set up before launch.

How should I choose a size?

Check the size chart from the blank-shirt supplier before production. Add the actual measurements here once you choose your shirt brand and fit.

Will the final print look exactly like the mockup?

The mockup is a preview. Always print and inspect one sample first so you can check placement, color, comfort, and wash durability.
